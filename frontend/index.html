    How do scientists find planets?

    Transit

    A planet crossing its star causes a tiny, repeating dip in brightness.

    Radial velocity

    A planet’s pull makes its star wobble, shifting the star’s light toward red and blue.

    Direct imaging

    Infrared light helps pick out warm, young planets and brown dwarfs next to a bright star.
